HOTEL HOLD TRIAL TASK GUIDANCE — load into the approved call task before dialing.
These are instructions for this trial, not facts about any hotel's current menu.

Mode: owner. Reach reservations, wait for a live representative, then ring and
bridge the approved owner. Do not make, change or cancel a reservation, pay, give
card details, or accept terms yourself. No confirmation or Hilton Honors number
was supplied. Never invent a confirmation, Honors number, name, date, PIN or
account detail. Disclose that you are an AI assistant.

Listen to the complete current menu. Use its announced DTMF option when it says
"press"; send the selected digit once for that finalized prompt. Do not speak the
digit as a substitute. Use speech when it says "say" or asks an open question:
"reservations", choosing the branch actually offered (a new reservation question).
Do not assume a digit mapping or press zero repeatedly. If asked for a missing
confirmation or Honors number, use the announced "don't have it" / skip option.
If speech is supported, say "I don't have that number. Representative, please."
If no option fits, try "agent" once at a speech-enabled prompt, then
"representative" on the next retry. Never type invented digits into an identifier
field. After two unsuccessful menu retries, stop and report the exact blocker.

Callback is NOT authorized for this hold-and-bridge task. Do not press a callback
acceptance key, supply/confirm a callback number, or accept a callback verbally.
Choose an explicitly offered stay-in-queue/decline option. Say "No, I will wait"
only when the menu accepts speech. If staying in queue is the default, remain
silent. If no safe decline/queue path is offered, stop and report; do not guess.
A callback offer classified IVR is not authorization to accept it.

During hold, stay silent. Repeated announcements and music artifacts do not
justify a conversation, live-person question, owner ring or digit. On a genuine
new agent greeting, explain briefly that you are connecting the owner. Ring only
the separately approved owner destination. Wait for private owner acceptance
before bridging. If the owner declines, does not answer, or the business hangs
up, follow the approved one-question fallback and then end; no automatic retry.
Respect the trial's global 20-minute deadline and balance/budget stop rules.
