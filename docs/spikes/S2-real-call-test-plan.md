**V2 — Fi forwarding target verification, ≤2 minutes, same setup bucket.** Do immediately before R7 (not before outbound-Fi tests). Can adds only the new Twilio local number in Fi. Prefer verify-by-call to avoid unbudgeted SMS assumptions. Minute 0–1: incoming verification reaches a temporary owner-controlled verification handler, isolated from Hermes transcripts. **R1 — Outbound identity, voice loop and barge-in; ≤4 minutes.** Twilio → Spectrum, with verified Fi caller ID. ## Offline hold simulation fixture plan (zero calls)

This is a specification; no audio/fixture files are written by S2 because write scope is Markdown only. Future implementation uses existing Python/stdlib/local tooling; no paid model, download or cloud transcription is needed for deterministic replay.

- Fixture manifest: version, author/license (self-authored), sample rate/channels for audio, SHA-256, language, scenario duration, ordered timestamped events and ground-truth intervals. Use small locally spoken recordings only with the participant's authorization; otherwise start with authored transcript streams. No captured business calls or copyrighted hold music. Silence/waveform tones can be generated locally; do not call a service to source them.
- Transcript event format: `at_ms`, `type` (prompt/interrupt/dtmf/socket/status), `turn_id`, `text`, `last`, `label`, `human_onset_ms`, expected permissible actions. Runtime labels are hidden from classifier. Include partial/final replacements, duplicated events and reconnect replay. Short verification events contain `[MASKED]` rather than any code.
- Build at least 60 distinct short sequences: 10 music/silence/no-transcript, 10 recurring recorded hold announcements, 10 IVR prompts, 10 voicemail greetings (including “hello” plus delayed instructions), 10 live humans (quiet/short/long greetings and accents), 10 adversarial/UNKNOWN mixtures. Split by script/speaker, not adjacent windows: 30 development and 30 held-out. Avoid identical phrase/speaker leakage into held-out tests.
- Add six 35-minute soak scripts (3.5 hours total; calculate negative hold exposure from labels): repeated announcements, long silence, speech over music, recorded conversational “hello,” a late human at minute 32, and disconnect/replay near the human transition. Use a virtual clock for routine tests and one real-time soak for watchdog/memory growth. Repetition creates duration coverage, not independent statistical examples.
- Compare deterministic transcript rules, cached rules + Hermes adapter with canned structured answers, and optional Jev-compatible decision schema without a live provider call. Actual Hermes semantic accuracy remains **UNCONFIRMED** until an authorized existing-model run; canned answers test orchestration only. Do not report a canned classifier's confusion matrix as model quality.
- For local acoustic work, retain separate WAV + labels and μ-law/8 kHz renderings matching Twilio's transport format ([Media Streams messages](https://www.twilio.com/docs/voice/media-streams/websocket-messages), accessed 2026-11-04). Vary levels/noise, phrase pauses and music overlap. Local VAD/beep/repetition experiments are optional; they are not inputs available from Relay. Offline transcript tests cannot validate cloud STT, TTS, PSTN DTMF or audio latency; R1–R3/R8 cover those boundaries.
- Score event and interval confusion matrices over HOLD_MUSIC, HOLD_MESSAGE, IVR, VOICEMAIL, HUMAN and UNKNOWN. Report per-class precision/recall, false HUMAN/owner-ring events per hold-hour, missed human episodes, detection delays from acoustic and recognized onset, UNKNOWN fraction and semantic calls per hold-hour. Ground truth comes from hidden authored schedules/human labels, never from the classifier itself.
- Proposed release gates: zero false owner rings across all negative held-out/soak fixtures; no duplicate dial/digits on replay; no missed eligible human episode in the small held-out suite; all recognized human confirmation delays ≤5 s; no cumulative context/memory growth across the real-time soak. These are targets, not measured results or confidence guarantees. Failure keeps detection manual/experimental until fixed and rerun.
- Offline failure injection also covers invalid/missing/replayed signatures; foreign account/session; bridge accept after business ended; owner voicemail without acceptance; stale conference callbacks; all hangup entry points; budget exhausted; destination normalization/emergency variants and unsupported country paths. Assert rejected dial paths invoke no provider adapter. Never “test” emergency blocking with a real call.
